What We Deliver

A Complete AI Growth Engine

01

Automated Property & Roof Qualification

Every lead is intelligently verified before reaching your sales team. Our AI evaluates satellite imagery, roof orientation, shading, ownership status, estimated energy consumption, and utility requirements — eliminating renters and poor-fit properties before they ever enter your pipeline.

02

24/7 AI Lead Engagement

The moment someone becomes a lead, our AI engages them across SMS, WhatsApp, website chat, and voice. It instantly answers solar questions, explains available incentives, qualifies motivation, and even reads uploaded electricity bills to calculate accurate energy requirements. Your business never misses another opportunity.

03

Instant Solar Savings & ROI Estimates

Prospects receive personalized financial projections within minutes. Using their address and utility information, our AI creates estimated panel layouts, projected monthly savings, payback periods, and long-term 25-year ROI — building confidence before your sales call even begins.

04

Autonomous Appointment Booking

Forget chasing leads. Our AI schedules qualified consultations directly into your team's calendar, sends reminders, handles rescheduling, confirms attendance, and nurtures prospects before every meeting — dramatically reducing no-shows.

05

Deep CRM Automation

Every interaction is automatically synchronized with your CRM. Conversation history, qualification status, roof reports, utility analysis, appointment details, and AI lead scores are pushed into HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Salesforce, OpenSolar, and other major platforms — ensuring your team always focuses on the highest-value opportunities first.
